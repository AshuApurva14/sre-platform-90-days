# Final Mental Model

```bash

User reports problem
        ↓
What is the symptom?
        ↓
Is the process running?
        ↓
Is the port available?
        ↓
Does the application respond?
        ↓
Are permissions correct?
        ↓
Are CPU/memory normal?
        ↓
What do the logs show?
        ↓
Take the safest corrective action
        ↓
Verify recovery
        ↓
Document what happened
        ↓
Automate repeated checks

```
