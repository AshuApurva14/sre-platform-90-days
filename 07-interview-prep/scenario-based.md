# Scenario-Based Interview Practice

## Scenarios

1. A deployment is green but users receive 5xx responses. What do you check?
2. A pod is in CrashLoopBackOff. How do you investigate?
3. Latency increased immediately after a release. What evidence do you collect?
4. A Kubernetes service cannot be reached. How do you isolate DNS vs Service vs Pod vs Ingress?
5. Your error budget is almost exhausted. What changes in your engineering priorities?
6. How would you test whether a pod-kill failure is recoverable?
